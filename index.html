Balance & Bliss

Psychotherapy for understanding yourself, your relationships and the patterns you carry.

Balance & Bliss is a platform dedicated to supporting emotional balance, mental clarity, and long-term psychological well-being. Through holistic, evidence-based, and compassionate care, the aim is to help individuals build resilience, embrace their authentic selves, and create meaningful change.

This is a warm, safe, and trauma-informed space for adults navigating emotional difficulties, relationship concerns, challenging life experiences, self-criticism, attachment patterns, and questions of identity and purpose. My client-centered approach honours each individual’s unique experiences and supports healing through self-awareness, self-compassion, and personal growth.

Balance does not mean life is always calm, and bliss does not mean difficulties disappear. Therapy offers an opportunity to understand past experiences, recognise existing patterns, and develop a healthier relationship with yourself, allowing you to navigate life with greater awareness, flexibility, compassion, and inner peace.

Meet Aniket and his process

Aniket is a dedicated mental health professional and psychotherapist who supports adults aged 20–50 through life transitions, complex trauma, anxiety, attachment wounds, and relationship difficulties. His approach recognises the person behind every struggle, creating a safe, compassionate space to understand experiences and encourage emotional healing.

His therapeutic process is collaborative, client-centred, trauma-informed, and integrative. Through individual and couples therapy, he helps clients recognise emotional patterns, develop self-awareness, build self-compassion, and make meaningful choices aligned with their values and goals.

Aniket holds a Master’s in Counselling Psychology from S.P. College, Pune, and a Diploma in Family and Marriage Counselling from MKSSS Baya Karve, Pune. His professional experience includes psychotherapy, counselling, school counselling, remedial teaching, and workshops, supported by specialised training in trauma-informed care, Emotionally Focused Therapy, somatic psychology, attachment, and other therapeutic approaches.

Therapy is not a destination for us to reach, but it is a process where we explore, understand and work on various aspects which create distress. Therapy is a two way relationship, both you as my client and I will be responsible. As a client in therapy, you have certain rights that are important for you to know; and as a therapist, I have corresponding responsibilities to you.
My responsibilities as a therapist-
1. Whatever we talk and you share with me, all that information will be kept confidential.
2. If there are signs of self harm or harm to others, then with your permission and by letting you know, the confidentiality will be broken to a person with whom you feel safe and comfortable.
3. I'll make sure that the 1 hour of the session feels safe and comforting to you to share, vent out, be vulnerable, and take the steps ahead to achieve healthy well-being.
Your responsibilities as a client-
1. Turning up for the session at the decided time slot and day/ date.
2. Paying for every session in advance.
3. If due to any reason you want to reschedule the appointment, you have to inform me 24 hours before the session.
