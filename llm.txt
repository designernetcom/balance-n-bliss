LLM PROMPTS & ASSETS FOR SEO - AI INDUSTRY RANKING
Language: English
Objective: Provide optimized prompts and structured content to rank high on Google AI, OpenAI, Cloud AI, and Perplexity AI search visibility.

TARGET KEYWORDS (Primary & Variations)
- Google AI solutions
- OpenAI services
- Cloud AI platforms
- Perplexity AI tools
- best AI company
- AI-powered business solutions
- AI consulting services
- AI chatbot development
- enterprise AI integration
- machine learning solutions
- AI for business automation

INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE LLM (Usage Guidelines)
1. Write in clear, authoritative, professional tone to build trust in AI expertise.
2. Incorporate primary AI-related keywords naturally into titles, headings, and first 100 words of each page.
3. Create meta titles (50-60 chars) and meta descriptions (150-160 chars) optimized for clicks and CTR.
4. Produce service page content (300-500 words each) with keyword-rich subheadings (H2/H3).
5. Generate blog outlines and content briefs targeting AI trends, comparisons (Google AI vs OpenAI), and practical use cases.
6. Provide structured JSON-LD schema: Organization, Product, FAQ, and BreadcrumbList.
7. Supply image alt tags with descriptive AI terms (e.g., “Cloud AI integration dashboard”).
8. Add internal linking suggestions for services, case studies, and blog posts.

PAGE-BY-PAGE PROMPTS

Homepage
Prompt:
"Write 3 meta title variations and 3 meta description variations for an AI solutions company homepage. Keywords: 'Google AI', 'OpenAI services', 'Cloud AI platforms'. Add a 30-40 word hero tagline and a 120-150 word company introduction positioning expertise in enterprise AI, business automation, and AI consulting."

About Page
Prompt:
"Create an About Us page (200-250 words) highlighting expertise in AI consulting, partnerships with Google AI and OpenAI, cloud-based AI deployments, and team credentials. Add 5 bullet points covering industry experience, certifications, client impact, innovation, and global reach."

Services Page (Overview)
Prompt:
"Write an SEO-optimized overview (150-200 words) of AI services. Include sub-sections with 40-60 word blurbs for: AI Consulting, Cloud AI Integration, OpenAI-Powered Chatbots, Machine Learning Models, AI Business Automation. Naturally embed keywords like 'Cloud AI platforms' and 'OpenAI services'."

Individual Service Pages (Template)
Prompt:
"For each service page, generate:
- Meta title and description
- H1 with service keyword
- 300-400 word service explanation (benefits, process, industries served)
- 5 FAQs for schema markup
Example services: AI Consulting, AI Chatbot Development, Enterprise Cloud AI, Machine Learning Models, AI for Business Automation."

Contact Page
Prompt:
"Meta tags and short contact section including strong CTAs: 'Request a Demo', 'Schedule Consultation', 'Partner with Us'. Provide schema-ready NAP block with global office presence."

BLOG POST PROMPTS
Prompt:
"Write a blog outline and intro for 'Google AI vs OpenAI: Which AI Platform Suits Your Business?'. Include 6 H2 sections, pros/cons table, internal links to Services pages, and 3 suggested images with keyword-rich alt tags."

IMAGE ALT TAGS
Prompt:
"Generate alt tags (max 10 words) for AI images: 'Google AI analytics dashboard', 'OpenAI chatbot interface', 'Cloud AI platform demo', 'Perplexity AI research tools'."

INTERNAL LINKING GUIDELINES
- Link service pages to related case studies and blog posts.
- Ensure homepage links to all core services and top blogs.
- Use contextual anchors like 'enterprise AI solutions', 'AI consulting services'.
