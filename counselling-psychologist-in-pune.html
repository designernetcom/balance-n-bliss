What to expect

What Happens in a Session — No Surprises

One of the biggest reasons people delay therapy is not knowing what they're walking into. Here's exactly how a session at Balance & Bliss works.

  • Intake call (free 15 mins)
    A brief call to understand what brought you here, answer your questions and confirm we're the right fit. No commitment required at this stage.
  • First session
    mapping the picture, we spend 60 minutes understanding your history, current challenges and goals not diagnosing, just listening and building a clear starting point together.
  • Structured sessions: real work begins
    Every 6–8 sessions, we step back and review what's shifted and what hasn't. Therapy should have a direction. we make sure it does. The sessions flow with addressing the unaddressed emotional experiences of the past and working on overcoming the present-day concerns.
