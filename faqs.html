Frequently asked questions (FAQs)

01

Do I need to know exactly what is wrong before starting therapy?

No. You can begin with a feeling of being stuck, a recurring concern or a desire to understand yourself better. We can explore what brings you to therapy together.

02

Do I need a mental-health diagnosis?

No diagnosis is required simply to enquire about psychotherapy. Your concerns and support needs can be explored to determine whether the service is appropriate.

03

What happens during the first session?

We discuss what brings you to therapy, your current concerns, relevant background and what you hope to work towards. You can ask questions and share any concerns about the process.

04

Can I decide what I want to discuss?

Yes. Therapy is collaborative. You can raise what feels important, ask questions and discuss the pace of the work. Some topics may be explored gradually.

Sessions and progress

Individual sessions are approximately 60 minutes. The duration of couples sessions should be confirmed when enquiring.

Frequency is discussed collaboratively and may depend on your needs, goals, availability and how the work develops. Sessions may be weekly or fortnightly where appropriate.

There is no fixed number that applies to everyone. This depends on your concerns, goals, circumstances and the nature of the therapeutic work. Progress and ongoing needs can be reviewed together.

It is important to be able to discuss concerns about the process. You can tell me if something feels uncomfortable, unclear or unhelpful so that we can consider how to proceed.

Services

Confidentiality and safety

Confidentiality is an important part of psychotherapy. Its scope and limits are explained through informed consent before the therapeutic work begins. Exceptions may apply in relevant safety situations or where disclosure is legally required.

Personal information and therapy records are handled according to the applicable confidentiality, privacy and professional requirements. Specific arrangements, including any relevant limits, will be explained before you begin.

If your needs fall outside the scope of the service, we can discuss appropriate referrals or additional support where possible.

Fees and appointments

Please contact Balance & Bliss for current individual and couples therapy fees.

Use the consultation enquiry form or contact Balance & Bliss directly. Your preferred appointment time will be confirmed subject to availability.

The cancellation, rescheduling and missed-session policy will be shared before appointments begin.
